# Replan

The home assistant flags the stress, proposes a recovery, and replans with the person's yes.

1. **Name the stress.** Which milestone, how far off, why, and what depends on it (from the schedule).
2. **Find the options.** For depth, send a sub-agent or a project chat one clear question with the Command Center lines and schedule rows it needs. Look at: cutting or deferring scope, moving an owner, running work in parallel, asking the client for a date, adding a person.
3. **Bring one recommendation**, with its cost (time, money, who it touches) and the one alternative worth considering. One question.
4. **On their yes:** update the schedule and Command Center, add a HANDOFF line, and draft any message to the client or team for the person to send.
5. **A war-room chat** fits a crunch of several days with daily moving parts (a launch, an event). Give the person the title ("ACM · War room · Launch") and a one-paragraph brief to paste. It reports through the journal like any project chat, and ends when the crunch does.
